# ZonaQuintas — provisional brand kit

Status: **partial / awaiting official logo and approved media**.

Website: https://zonaquintas.com/

## Product and audiences
Argentine marketplace for short-stay rental properties (quintas/casas de fin de semana). Two distinct audiences: renters searching for a property, and owners considering publishing a listing. Do not conflate these journeys.

## Confirmed palette (existing ZonaQuintas product tokens)
- Primary: `#33ff33`
- Primary dark: `#28a728`
- Tertiary: `#84b6f4`
These are product colors, not permission to invent a new logo or typeface.

## Creative territories
1. Renters: occasion-led stories (weekend with friends, family gathering) grounded in actual approved property imagery.
2. Owners: demonstrate the actual listing flow and explain value without promising earnings or occupancy.
3. Trust: explain verifiable platform steps and policies; do not imply a payment protection or verification feature without confirming its current implementation.

## Assets required
- Official logo as SVG or transparent PNG, uploaded by owner.
- Authorized property photos/videos, with usage permission and listing attribution as needed.
- Approved screen recordings of search, listing and booking flows, with sensitive information hidden.
- Official fonts/type treatments and any restricted uses of the logo.

## Tone
Argentine Spanish, approachable and specific. Avoid stock claims like “momentos inolvidables” without supporting visuals or an actual story.
