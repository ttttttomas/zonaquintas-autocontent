## Product and audience
iWeb offers website and software development. Priority product: Tranett, a management platform for travel agencies, particularly wholesale/tour operators. Secondary offers: custom business systems and websites.

## Editorial priorities
1. Tranett: real workflows (departures, bookings, passengers, rooms, payments, settlements) only after verifying feature state with source or approved demo.
2. Agency pain points: show the business problem first, then a specific and demonstrable workflow.
3. iWeb: real examples of systems/websites, measured benefits only when documented.

## Brand source of truth
Extract the **actual** logo, fonts, colors, and visual rules from the official site's source/assets and have the owner approve them. **Do not reuse the current arbitrary dark-blue gradient as official brand identity.** Do not fabricate logo assets.

## Assets required
- Verified logo URL or original SVG/PNG from the official site, locally vendored with provenance.
- Current UI screenshots or recordings of Tranett and other systems, fully anonymized.
- Approved client/project names and testimonials (if any); default is no client identification.
- Product claims verified against current release.

## Tone
Clear Argentine Spanish, B2B, practical and credible. Avoid generic “transformá tu negocio” claims.
